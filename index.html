Pork Adobo

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 kilo pork (liempo or kasim), cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 whole garlic, crushed
  • 1 medium onion, sliced (optional)
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS STEP BY STEP

  1. Wash and cut the pork into bite-sized pieces. Mix it with soy sauce and crushed garlic. Let it marinate for 15–30 minutes if you have time./li>
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a pot. Add the pork and cook for a few minutes until the outside starts to brown.
  3. Pour in the marinade and add the bay leaves and peppercorns. Add the sliced onion if you want.
  4. Pour in the vinegar and water. Bring it to a boil without stirring for about 3–5 minutes to let the vinegar cook.
  5. Lower the heat, cover the pot, and simmer for about 45–60 minutes. Stir occasionally after the vinegar has boiled. Add a little water if the sauce becomes too dry.
  6. Taste the sauce and add a little sugar if desired. Simmer uncovered for a few more minutes if you want a thicker sauce. Make sure the pork is fully cooked and tender, then serve hot with steamed rice.
